Why Indians choose DailyDose+ over generic pill reminders

Most global reminder apps treat every dose as identical. DailyDose+ is designed around how medicines are actually taken in India — with breakfast, after lunch, before bed, and across multi-week courses like antibiotics or Metformin.

  • notifications_activeMeal-synced alerts — reminders tied to “with breakfast” or “after dinner”, not random times.
  • edit_calendarPrescription duration — set number of days, start and end dates, and see “Day 12 of 30” progress.
  • water_dropWater + medicine together — hydration tracking with nudges when your medication needs extra fluids.
  • family_restroomFamily Mode — manage your parents’ schedules from your phone; large UI for seniors.
  • lunch_diningMealOBox Sync — optional link to MealOBox tiffins so meals avoid drug–food conflicts.
  • lockEncrypted vault — prescriptions and lab reports stored with AES-256 encryption. Learn about security.

How to set up your first reminder

  1. Download DailyDose+ from Google Play (free).
  2. Add your medicine name, dose, and timing — e.g. Metformin 500mg after breakfast.
  3. Set course length in days and start date; the app calculates your end date.
  4. Enable notifications and optionally Family Mode for dependents.
